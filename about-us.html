Who I Am

Meet Richard Ryan Alby.

I'm Richard Ryan Alby — a professional book marketer, creative strategist, and the founder of Richard Ryan Alby Book Marketing. My work centres on helping authors transform their publishing ambitions into visible, well-positioned realities.

I believe that the space between a finished manuscript and a successful book is not luck — it's strategy, branding, consistent promotion, and a deep understanding of who your readers are and where to find them. That space is where I work.

My approach is rooted in thoughtful, author-focused marketing. I don't believe in cookie-cutter campaigns or generic promotional templates. Every book is unique, every author's voice is different, and every marketing plan I develop reflects that individuality.

I founded this business because I saw talented authors pouring years of dedication into their writing, only to find themselves lost in the noise of an overcrowded marketplace. Professional book marketing changes that.

Whether you're preparing to publish your first novel, building a series, transitioning from traditional to independent publishing, or simply looking for a professional partner who understands the world of books — I'm here to help you develop a marketing presence that reflects the quality of your writing.

Experience
My Experience

Years of Dedication to the Craft of Book Marketing.

Over the course of my career, I have developed a deep understanding of what it takes to market books effectively in today's competitive publishing environment. My experience spans across multiple genres, publishing formats, and promotional channels.

I've worked with debut authors navigating the uncertainty of their first launch, experienced writers seeking to refresh their brand presence, and ambitious independents building entire marketing ecosystems around their creative output.

This breadth of experience has given me a practical, grounded perspective on what works — and what doesn't — in book marketing. I combine strategic thinking with creative instinct, always tailoring my approach to the specific book, audience, and author behind every project I take on.

My expertise covers book marketing strategy, author branding, social media marketing, book launch campaigns, promotional content creation, digital visibility, and long-term author platform development.

My Purpose

Why I'm Passionate About Helping Authors Succeed.

Books have the power to educate, inspire, challenge, comfort, and transform. But behind every published book is an author who invested months — sometimes years — of creative energy, emotional commitment, and intellectual effort. That investment deserves to be honoured with marketing that is just as thoughtful and intentional.

My passion for book marketing comes from a genuine love of literature and a deep respect for the creative process. I understand the vulnerability involved in sharing your work with the world, and I approach every project with the sensitivity and professionalism that relationship demands.

I'm driven by the belief that professional marketing should be accessible to all authors — not just those backed by major publishing houses. Independent and self-published authors deserve the same calibre of strategic promotion, and I'm committed to providing it.

A Genuine Love for Books

Literature has shaped my worldview. Helping authors share their stories with the world feels like a natural extension of that love.

Championing Independent Authors

Every writer — regardless of their publishing path — deserves access to professional marketing support that takes their work seriously.

Bridging the Gap Between Writing and Readership

Marketing is the bridge that connects an author's creative vision with the audience who needs to experience it.

How I Work

A Clear, Considered Approach to Every Project.

My process is designed to create clarity from the beginning. I take the time to understand your book, your goals, your audience, and your vision before developing any marketing strategy. Every step is intentional, every deliverable is purposeful, and every conversation is collaborative.

Here's how a typical marketing engagement unfolds:

01

Discovery

We begin with a detailed conversation about your book, your publishing goals, your intended audience, and what success looks like for you.

02

Research & Strategy

I research your genre landscape, audience behaviour, and competitive positioning to develop a marketing strategy tailored to your specific needs.

03

Creative Development

From branding assets to promotional content, social media graphics, and campaign messaging — every creative element is developed with precision.

04

Execution & Delivery

Campaigns are launched, content is deployed, and marketing materials are delivered — all according to a coordinated timeline you can rely on.
